# 🎮 UPLB Game Development Society

**The premier game development organization of the University of the Philippines Los Baños.**

We are a community of game development enthusiasts united by our passion for video games as an art form and medium of expression. We aim to make game development more accessible to students by fostering a space where creativity, collaboration, and learning thrive.

### 🚀 What We Do

- **Learn & Grow** – Provide opportunities for members to develop their skills in game design, programming, and asset creation.
- **Create & Collaborate** – Develop games and bring creative ideas to life through teamwork.
- **Showcase & Promote** – Celebrate games created by our members and local Filipino game developers.
- **Build Community** – Foster appreciation for game development and help grow its culture in UPLB and beyond.

### 🎯 Our Vision

To be the premier game development organization in UPLB, making the field of game development more accessible to its students.
